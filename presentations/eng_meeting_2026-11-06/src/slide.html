Approximately 3 minutes. Press S for this view. Blue bars are targets; purple bars are open-ended research and development. (20 s) This roadmap covers three related projects through March: the scheduler refactor, the motion planning refactor, and research in multi-agent path finding. Hito support continues alongside them. (60 s) Scheduler, task planning. The objective is to make the scheduler safe to change. The planning tests are now recorded scenario files (PR 1007), so every change must reproduce the current plans exactly. PR 1018, merged yesterday, restructured the scheduler into one file per pipeline stage and fixed fourteen latent defects. Each stage has merged within about a week, so the remaining stages should largely complete by the end of the year: the tray solver interface, the AGV assignment module and the follow-up cleanup, with benchmarks in January. (35 s) Motion planning. I studied the motion planner in October and will review it in depth from mid-November to early December. In January I intend to refactor and redesign its interfaces. The direction is a more conventional problem description: tasks as sequences of goals assigned to agents, with constraints between goals across tasks, while preserving the event sequencing the scheduler depends on. The design is not final. (25 s) Research. Preliminary notes exist; focused work begins in December. From February, all three areas move to research and development: translating published algorithms into code we can validate on site. No specific method has been selected. (30 s) The right-hand panel lists candidate directions for evaluation: tray rearrangement as a puzzle-based storage problem, off-peak vehicle pre-positioning, searching over AGV priority orders, repairing a failed plan rather than rejecting the job, reordering AGVs at run time when one is delayed, and a scalable fallback planner. (10 s) Hito: I delivered the pause and e-stop policy in October and continue to provide support as required.
